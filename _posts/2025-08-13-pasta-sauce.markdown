<br/>

Serves 4

* 1 onion diced
* 2 cloves garlic
* 3–4 pīman or 1 green bell pepper (paprika)

* 200~300g Ground Pork/Beef

* 800g canned chopped tomatos

* ~1/4 tsp oregano
* ~1/4 tsp thyme
* 2 tsp salt
* 0.5 tsp basil
* black pepper
* 3 dried red chillies (or red pepper flakes / powder)
