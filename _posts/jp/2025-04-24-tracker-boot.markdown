# From the Ashes of Trackers Past … Comes Tracker Boot!

**[trackerboot.com](https://trackerboot.com/)**

* Launching April 25, 2025.
* Kotlin Spring + TypeScript React + GraphQL = slick, scalable, and snappy
* live update and optimistic data processing
* company-wide analytics to actually know what's happening.
* Built by people who built Pivotal Tracker

Already, 334,300 stories have migrated from the old world to Tracker Boot.
Be part of the *Great Backlog Migration*

## Be Agile with Tracker Boot
You don’t need a credit card, a sales call, or your manager’s approval.
Just sign up, poke around, and see if Tracker Boot makes your heart flutter.

```
Accelerating Teams since 1989
Tracker Boot traces its roots to Pivotal Tracker,
the product management tool developed by Pivotal Labs,
a Silicon Valley pioneer in Agile development.
Now, Tracker Boot continues that legacy. Developed by Bekind Labs,
founded by former Pivotal Labs team members,
it inherits Pivotal Tracker's core functionalities
while enabling seamless data migration for a smooth transition.
Built for rapid releases, continuous iteration, and adaptability to change,
Tracker Boot empowers product teams to deliver value faster and more efficiently.
```
