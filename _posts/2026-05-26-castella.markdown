<ol>
    <li>Gather the ingredients
        <ol>
            <li>All purpose flour (100g)</li>
            <li>Sugar (100g)</li>
            <li>Eggs 4</li>
            <li>Mirin (2 Tablespoons / 30mL)</li>
            <li>Honey (2 Tablespoons / 30mL)</li>
            <li>Oil (2 Tablespoons / 30mL)</li>
        </ol>
    </li>
    <li>Line an 18cm×18cm pan with parchment paper</li>
    <li>Sift the flour (100g) into a separate bowl for later</li>
    <li>Beat eggs (4) and sugar (100g) in a bowl partially submerged in an additional bowl of 40°C water
        until ribbons
    </li>
    <li>Preheat oven to 180°C</li>
    <li>Add additional wet ingredients and mix at low speed
        <ol>
            <li>Mirin (2 Tablespoons / 30mL)</li>
            <li>Honey (2 Tablespoons / 30mL)</li>
            <li>Oil (2 Tablespoons / 30mL)</li>
        </ol>
    </li>
    <li>Fold in the flour</li>
    <li>Pour into lined baking pan</li>
    <li>Drop the pan from ~10cm onto a hard countertop several times to release bubbles</li>
    <li>Bake 180°C for 10min then 140°C for 40min</li>
</ol>
